# Daily and focus mode

Daily is the page for today. It shows your meetings for today and tomorrow, the todos that matter now, and the projects and areas you have in focus.

## What appears on Daily

A todo appears on Daily when it is open and one of these is true:

- it is due today, tomorrow or overdue
- it is marked **Next** and its project or area is in daily, weekly or monthly focus
- it is in the Inbox and marked **Next**

Todos marked Waiting for, Someday or Maybe never appear on Daily. Todos are grouped by project or area.

The left column shows meetings with the tabs **Today** and **Tomorrow** (and **Google** when the Google Calendar integration is on).

## Daily focus

Pick which projects and areas you are working on today in the focus panel. The number of focus slots is set in **Settings → Focus** (default: 3 projects and 2 areas). A full list stays full until you remove one - there is no automatic swapping. Focus stays until you remove it.

## Filtering by a tag

Tap a tag inside a todo or meeting (for example `#lena`) to show only items with that tag. If the tag is the abbreviation of a project or area (for example `#GR`), Daily shows all of that project's items. Tap the tag again to remove the filter.

## Focus mode

Focus mode is for the few things you are on the hook for right now.

1. Mark todos with `#now`: double-tap the **Next** badge of a todo, or tap **Focus mode** on Daily and pick from the Next todos of your focused projects and areas, then **Start focus session**.
2. In focus mode the tabs disappear. You see Quick Add, your `#now` todos and meetings in the next four hours - nothing else.
3. Checked-off todos stay visible until the last one is done. Then the set clears and you can pick a new one - or not.
4. Leave focus mode with the small exit mark in the corner.

If you close the plugin while in focus mode, it opens in focus mode again next time.
