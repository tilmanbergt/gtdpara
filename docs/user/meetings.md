# Meetings

A meeting has a date, an optional time or length, and a title that can carry tags. Meetings live in the file of their project or area, or in the Inbox.

## Where meetings show up

- **Daily**: today and tomorrow
- **Week**: the week grid; tap a day for the full list
- **Month**: only monthly highlights (the **M** mark); tap a day for all its meetings
- **Current**: all meetings of that project or area, upcoming and past

## Meeting notes

Tap the note icon on a meeting to create its note. gtdpara fills it according to the matching [Tag Rule](note-templates.md): for example title, date, time and the open todos that share a tag with the meeting. Until the end of the meeting (for a meeting without a time: the end of its day), this information is refreshed each time you open the note from gtdpara. After that the note is left as it is.

Instead of one note per meeting, a Tag Rule can put the notes of all matching meetings as pages into one shared note per project, for example one "Team sync" note with a page per meeting.

## Preparation and review

For meetings that need preparing or following up, switch on **Prepare before** and/or **Review after** in the meeting's Tag Rule. The meeting row then shows a small mark:

- **P** before the meeting, until you tap it (it becomes `#prepped`)
- **R** after the meeting, until you tap it (it becomes `#reviewed`)
- a check mark when the current phase is done

Past meetings that still need a review appear in the Review step **Meetings to close out** (last 7 days).

## Cancelling and repeating

The trash icon in the edit form cancels a meeting; it stays in the file, marked as cancelled. gtdpara has no repeating meetings: for a series, use **New from this** to create the next one with the same title, and give the series its own tag (for example `#team-sync`).

## From Google Calendar

With the experimental Google Calendar integration you can copy calendar events into gtdpara meetings. See [Google Calendar and Gmail](integrations.md).
