# Weekly Review

The Review tab walks you through a weekly review, one step at a time. You can also do a single step whenever you like.

## The overview

The first page lists all steps. Each row shows how many things are in the step and when you last reviewed it. A step you haven't reviewed for more than a week gets a ● mark, and so does the Review tab itself. Steps with nothing in them count as done automatically.

Tap a step to open it. Inside a step:

- **Reviewed ›** saves the step as reviewed and goes to the next one
- **Skip ›** goes on without marking it reviewed
- **‹ Back** and **Overview** move around without saving

## The steps

- **Week ahead**: this week's meetings and focus; from Friday to Sunday the coming week
- **Meetings to close out**: past meetings (last 7 days) whose review is still open. A todo you add there records the meeting it came from, so it shows under **I owe** in that meeting's thread (the grey line in Quick Add, see [Threads](threads.md))
- **Tending threads**: the people, teams and series you work with (counterparts, see [Threads](threads.md)), per area or project. See below.
- **Gmail inbox**: only with the experimental Gmail integration, see [integrations](integrations.md)
- **Inbox to zero**: file or finish everything in the Inbox. Open lasso marks count too; the card **n marks to process** opens them, see [Lasso](lasso.md)
- **Stalled projects**: active projects without any open todo you can act on. Someday and Maybe todos don't count (Waiting for does); you can promote one of them to Next with one tap.
- **Done awaiting review**: projects marked Done - close them out or archive them
- **On Hold reconsideration**: paused projects and areas - keep them paused or reactivate them
- **Neglected areas**: the same for areas - no open todo you can act on
- **Unfocused next items**: projects and areas with a `#next` todo (without due date) but no daily, weekly or monthly focus. Add them to focus, or park the todo.
- **Focus reset**: choose weekly and monthly focus and goals. Switch between **Week** and **Month** at the top; each shows the same focus panel as the Week and Month tabs (tap an empty slot, then pick a project or area)

Steps that list several projects show them on the left; tap one to work on it on the right.

## Tending threads

This step is about keeping your word in ongoing relationships: is the next meeting set up, are agreements followed up, is anything waiting too long? It ranks nothing and raises no alarms.

On the left:

- **New** on top: counterparts you use but haven't confirmed, with the area or project they belong to, and inactive ones that got a new meeting (`back in use`).
- Then one block per area (with its projects) and per project without an area, its active counterparts in alphabetical order. The Inbox has none.

Each row has two lines: the counterpart, then `last 30.9. · next 14.10.` and what is open (`2 owe · w/f 18 d`). One-off counterparts (only `#wf/name` or `#owe/name`) show `w/f only`, `owe only` or `w/f · owe only` instead of meeting dates.

Tap a counterpart to see it on the right: Quick Add, then **Tend** and **Not** for a new one, **Open overview ›** and **Set inactive** for an active one, its next meetings (or **+ Next retro**, which puts the next meeting into Quick Add), what you owe, wait for and should raise, and its last three meetings. **Open overview ›** opens its thread overview over the Review; **‹ Review** brings you back to the step as you left it. **Set inactive** first goes through its open todos (Done, Cancel or Keep each one), see [Projects and Areas](projects-and-areas.md).

The recap counts counterparts confirmed, set inactive and next meetings added.

## The recap

Each step remembers what you did in it (items filed, todos done, focus changed). The overview shows the total of your last visits.
