# Weekly Review

The Review tab walks you through a weekly review, one step at a time. You can also do a single step whenever you like.

## The overview

The first page lists all steps. Each row shows how many things are in the step and when you last reviewed it. A step you haven't reviewed for more than a week gets a ● mark, and so does the Review tab itself. Steps with nothing in them count as done automatically.

Tap a step to open it. Inside a step:

- **Reviewed ›** saves the step as reviewed and goes to the next one
- **Skip ›** goes on without marking it reviewed
- **‹ Back** and **Overview** move around without saving

## The steps

- **Week ahead**: this week's meetings and focus; from Friday to Sunday the coming week
- **Meetings to close out**: past meetings (last 7 days) whose review is still open
- **Gmail inbox**: only with the experimental Gmail integration, see [integrations](integrations.md)
- **Inbox to zero**: file or finish everything in the Inbox
- **Stalled projects**: active projects without any open todo you can act on. Someday and Maybe todos don't count (Waiting for does); you can promote one of them to Next with one tap.
- **Done awaiting review**: projects marked Done - close them out or archive them
- **On Hold reconsideration**: paused projects and areas - keep them paused or reactivate them
- **Neglected areas**: the same for areas - no open todo you can act on
- **Unfocused next items**: projects and areas with a `#next` todo (without due date) but no daily, weekly or monthly focus. Add them to focus, or park the todo.
- **Focus reset**: choose weekly and monthly focus and goals

Steps that list several projects show them on the left; tap one to work on it on the right.

## The recap

Each step remembers what you did in it (items filed, todos done, focus changed). The overview shows the total of your last visits.
